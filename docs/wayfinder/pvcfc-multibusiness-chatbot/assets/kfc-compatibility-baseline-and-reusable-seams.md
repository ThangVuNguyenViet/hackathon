# KFC compatibility baseline and reusable seams

## Audit conclusion

The KFC implementation is already separated at several useful provider boundaries, but it is not yet a business-neutral runtime. Behavior compatibility means preserving the observable contracts below while moving KFC identity, vocabulary, catalog shape, content, presentation, fixtures, provider bindings, and acceptance inventory behind a Business Pack. The migration must not merely keep endpoints compiling: it must keep KFC's evidence-backed customer outcomes, authorization decisions, state transitions, confirmation rules, GenUI/text projections, persistence proofs, and all-or-nothing acceptance gate.

## Customer-visible outcome baseline

The repository-owned acceptance inventory defines 46 turns, executed in both GenUI and text modes (92 cases), rather than a single happy path (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityContracts.ts:4-18`). Its scenario ledger makes the following outcomes compatibility requirements:

| Outcome family | Required KFC behavior |
|---|---|
| Ordering and checkout | Build carts only from verified catalog codes/modifiers; quote a specific fulfillment location; validate vouchers; enumerate supported payment methods; collect invoice fields; preview, explicitly confirm, place, and create payment (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:27-64`). |
| Discovery and recommendations | Recommend menu/add-ons without mutating the cart, combine menu and promotion evidence, support exact-quantity/combo and modifier changes, and keep order placement forbidden until checkout (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:66-81`). |
| Availability, saved address, and stores | Do not mutate or quote from an unaccepted suggestion; after explicit selection, update the cart and quote the saved address; recheck availability; allow store discovery without silently changing fulfillment (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:83-122`). |
| Post-order service | Read status repeatedly from provider evidence; reject mutation of an already-submitted order; check status before cancellation/handoff; require confirmation before reordering while leaving the active order unchanged (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:124-138`). |
| Complaints and human support | Acknowledge complaints without commerce side effects, escalate only when justified/requested, and preserve later positive feedback without creating an order or payment (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:140-149`). |
| Natural Vietnamese and safety | Resolve noisy Vietnamese ordering, answer allergen questions through content/modifier evidence, clarify nonsense and ambiguous references, and refuse private employee contact without cart/order/payment side effects (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:151-161`). |
| Personalization and loyalty | Treat previous orders/favorites as suggestions requiring confirmation; expose points, history, rewards, wallet, and supported tools; require confirmation around loyalty write actions (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:163-192`). |
| Payment failure and abnormal orders | Check payment status rather than infer success, and route failed payment or abnormal-large-order cases to justified human support without extra commerce mutation (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:194-211`). |
| Payment-method questions | Answer from payment-method evidence while forbidding cart/order/payment mutation and the misleading post-order payment-status widget (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:213-219`). |

Customer language is also observable behavior: order, payment, restaurant, commerce, progress, support, and handoff states are translated to stable Vietnamese phrases and safe fallback phrases (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/customerLanguage.ts:3-94`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/customerLanguage.ts:95-125`).

## Safety baseline

1. **No implicit mutation.** `updateCart` requires an explicit mutation request, confirmation, or confirmed reorder; recent-order evidence alone cannot mutate the cart; an optional stricter gate requires `cartMutationConfirmed` (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:154-165`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:187-204`).
2. **Only verified items may mutate commerce state.** A code must be present in verified planning/search/cart/order evidence, and location-specific unavailability blocks mutation (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:34-42`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:206-220`). Customer favorites/recent orders are explicitly context, not consent (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:142-155`).
3. **Fulfillment is a prerequisite, not an assumption.** Ordering requires an address, store, method/disposition, a successful availability check, checked items, and no unavailable/timeslot-blocked items; a quote must match a customer-entered address draft or an explicitly accepted saved address (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:18-28`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:72-88`).
4. **Order placement is irreversible and explicitly confirmed.** `placeOrder` is blocked without `userConfirmedOrder` (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:182-185`). The confirmation is bound to environment, scenario, catalog observation/hash, cart, fulfillment, payment, and provider revisions (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/clients/interfaces.ts:29-49`). Atomic reservation/replay prevents duplicate irreversible execution and records a winning result before applying it (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/commerceExecution.ts:164-238`).
5. **Claims require evidence.** Promotion claims require a successful promotion tool; paid claims require an active order, paid state, and matching `checkPaymentStatus` evidence; allergen certainty is categorically blocked (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:226-241`).
6. **Handoff is bounded.** It is permitted for explicit handoff, complaint/safety intent, failed payment with the matching reason, or abnormal large orders, not as a generic recovery shortcut (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/safetyGates.ts:128-139`).
7. **Concurrent customer turns cannot create stale effects.** New Messenger/Zalo turns supersede only runs that have not crossed an irreversible boundary, and stale run generations cannot be claimed (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/agentRuns/coordinator.ts:27-69`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/agentRuns/coordinator.ts:106-166`).
8. **Cart mutation invalidates downstream proof.** Fulfillment, preview/order, payment, promotion, and invoice state are cleared after a real cart change (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/verifiedState.ts:144-169`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/verifiedState.ts:337-344`).

## Trusted identity baseline

`CustomerAccessContext` is trusted runtime authority and must never be populated from request payloads or model output. It carries tenant, surface/session subject, KFC subject, authentication and account-link states, time-bounded evidence, and authorized scopes (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/domain/types.ts:7-39`).

Access to customer data is fail-closed: the caller must be authenticated with verified, unexpired evidence; the KFC subject binding must be verified; session and customer must match; external channels must have a linked channel account; and the requested scope must be present (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/security/customerAccessContext.ts:58-130`). An unverified context defaults to tenant `kfc-vietnam`, unknown subjects, unauthenticated/unverified states, no evidence, and no scopes (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/security/customerAccessContext.ts:33-55`). Personalized state hydration therefore clears customer/reorder context when authorization fails and fetches saved addresses, favorites, and recent orders only after `customer:read` succeeds (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/verifiedState.ts:186-247`).

**Compatibility constraint:** a shared runtime may generalize the names of `tenantScope` and business subject identifiers, but it must preserve server-originated authority, expiry checks, channel-to-business subject binding, session/customer equality, per-capability scopes, and fail-closed clearing. A request-selected business identifier is routing input, not identity proof.

## State and evidence semantics

- Durable business state includes cart/address, order preview/order, pending reorder and catalog suggestion, fulfillment, promotions/content, customer context, payment, invoice, handoff, and tool trace (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/agentTurnState.ts:154-177`). Planner menu evidence is expressly turn-local and never persisted; durable fields are provider-verified snapshots (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/state.ts:39-76`).
- Every tool result records tool name, exact arguments, success, summary, and source provenance; successful state changes are applied through a single reducer and successful calls emit observable `tool_called` updates with boundary and provenance (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:349-377`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/verifiedState.ts:310-335`).
- Verified snapshots are reconstructed from append-only events and persisted after the turn; the snapshot is the durable source for later context, not model prose (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/verifiedState.ts:171-184`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/verifiedState.ts:278-308`).
- Source provenance distinguishes crawl/authenticated/mock/test/demo/provider runtime modes and carries a source file plus optional URL/API (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:20-36`). Content evidence additionally carries URL, file, retrieval/approval metadata, public audience, and content hash (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:237-250`).
- Live catalog evidence is pinned by environment, source URL, provider fingerprint, observation time/expiry, HTTP revision hints, content hash, and normalized items; revalidation fails if environment/provider or any referenced item changes (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/catalog/catalogObservation.ts:99-127`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/catalog/catalogObservation.ts:242-289`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/catalog/catalogObservation.ts:297-313`).

## GenUI and channel baseline

The KFC app channel uses a structured-companion GenUI profile; Messenger/Zalo use standalone social text, with GenUI forbidden there (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/responseProfile.ts:3-16`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/channelPresentation.ts:65-98`). Social channels may project trusted catalog media, while runtime assertions reject GenUI/social-media cross-contamination (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/channelPresentation.ts:100-127`).

The attachment contract has 13 stable widget kinds, lifecycle/status, data, actions, selection, and expiry; structured actions normalize back into Vietnamese customer commands such as add, address acceptance, order confirmation, payment, tracking, and handoff (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/genui/kfcGenUi.ts:1-49`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/genui/kfcGenUi.ts:70-115`). Widget selection is evidence/state driven: allergen requires content evidence, menus require current verified menu evidence, payment/tracking reconcile order and payment sources, fulfillment distinguishes missing/candidate/confirmed addresses, and checkout confirmation requires cart plus fulfillment (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/genui/kfcGenUiSelector.ts:128-215`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/genui/kfcGenUiSelector.ts:217-290`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/genui/kfcGenUiSelector.ts:353-416`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/genui/kfcGenUiSelector.ts:460-488`).

**Compatibility constraint:** Business Packs may choose widget content, labels, assets, and which supported widget maps to a lifecycle stage. They must not bypass the shared evidence/state predicates, inject GenUI into standalone-text channels, expose actions forbidden by the safety oracle, or turn a candidate address/payment status into a confirmed one.

## Evaluation gates

The canonical KFC gate is repository-owned and deterministic. Each turn specifies preconditions/evidence bindings, tool count/order/arguments, allowed mutable state and exact transitions, required/forbidden claims, GenUI shape/actions, Messenger parity, provider provenance, persistence/checkpoint evidence, latency, and required artifacts (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityContracts.ts:20-129`).

Evaluation fails on unexpected/missing tools or catalog/location/entity evidence (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityEvaluators.ts:174-275`), incorrect state transitions (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityEvaluators.ts:278-305`), forbidden text or incorrect/missing GenUI (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityEvaluators.ts:308-352`), missing provider provenance (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityEvaluators.ts:354-375`), or noncontiguous transcript/events/unreadable checkpoints (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityEvaluators.ts:378-421`). Acceptance is the conjunction of tool, state, grounded response, presentation, provider evidence, persistence, and latency scores; eligible text cases also add a semantic-response judge without weakening deterministic scores (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityEvaluators.ts:435-464`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityEvaluators.ts:482-537`).

GenUI proof additionally requires correct widgets aligned to live user turns, lifecycle coverage, a screenshot for every expected turn, no unexpected handoff, and concise widget responses of at most 420 characters (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/genUiProofEvaluator.ts:89-175`).

**Migration gate:** keep the existing KFC inventory name/version/digest and all KFC cases green before, during, and after extraction. Add business-neutral and PVCFC suites alongside it; do not replace, rename, reduce, or reinterpret the KFC oracle until behavioral equivalence has been independently demonstrated.

## Reusable ports and seams

These are the strongest candidates for shared-runtime contracts:

1. **Provider client ports.** Menu, cart, recommendation, promotion, membership, inventory, store locator, fulfillment, content, invoice, OMS, payment, delivery, customer, loyalty, handoff, feedback, and channel clients are explicit interfaces collected by `ExternalClients` (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/clients/interfaces.ts:62-169`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/clients/interfaces.ts:175-217`).
2. **Tool boundary taxonomy.** Tools map to catalog, POS, routing, fulfillment, promotion, membership, content, invoice, OMS, payment, and handoff boundaries (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/toolBoundaries.ts:3-50`). The mapping is reusable even if a Business Pack exposes only a subset.
3. **Evidence/provenance envelopes.** `ToolResult`, `SourceProvenance`, `ToolTraceEntry`, and `ContentEvidence` are business-agnostic in shape and should remain mandatory at adapter boundaries (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/domain/types.ts:275-286`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:31-36`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:237-250`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:371-377`).
4. **Graph turn contract and checkpoint seam.** `AgentTurnInput` injects clients, store, dashboard, planner/composer, run guard, tracer, checkpointer, and trusted confirmation authority; output cleanly separates response text, presentation, state, pause/confirmation, and GenUI (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/agentTurnState.ts:45-106`).
5. **Persistence and verified-state reducer.** Snapshot load/build/persist and centralized tool-result application are reusable patterns, provided Business Packs own the extensions to durable state (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/verifiedState.ts:171-184`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/verifiedState.ts:278-335`).
6. **Channel presentation capabilities.** Structured companion versus standalone text/media is a reusable channel capability model (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/channelPresentation.ts:6-26`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/channelPresentation.ts:46-78`).
7. **Run coordination and irreversible replay.** Debounced/coalesced external turns, generation guards, protected phases, and atomic irreversible reservations are reusable independent of KFC menu semantics (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/agentRuns/coordinator.ts:27-103`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/commerceExecution.ts:47-111`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/graph/commerceExecution.ts:164-238`).
8. **Evaluation schema/evaluator.** The oracle shape and conjunctive scoring model can be parameterized by business while retaining KFC cases unchanged (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityContracts.ts:90-155`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityEvaluators.ts:435-464`).

## Hard-coded KFC assumptions that belong in a Business Pack

- **Identity and routing:** channel union contains `kfc`; trusted access uses `kfcSubjectRef`; unverified contexts hard-code tenant `kfc-vietnam` and surface `kfc-app-chat` (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/domain/types.ts:4-5`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/domain/types.ts:27-39`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/security/customerAccessContext.ts:33-55`).
- **Domain vocabulary and commerce schema:** VND fields, pickup/delivery dispositions, KFC menu/combo/modifier composition, voucher/membership/payment method types, and the fixed KFC tool catalog are embedded in shared-looking types (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:27-30`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:74-109`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:128-140`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:208-275`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/types.ts:278-310`).
- **Catalog adapter:** the source schema assumes KFC menu/category/product fields, Vietnamese locale key `vn`, VND prices, KFC static image URL construction, POS IDs, quick combos, and nested modifier rules (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/catalog/catalogObservation.ts:28-97`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/catalog/catalogObservation.ts:177-214`).
- **Fixture corpus and clock:** the runtime imports a fixed KFC fixture bundle, uses the Hồ Chí Minh timezone, emits KFC API fallback URLs, and hard-wires KFC payment fixture IDs (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/fixtures/bundledFixtures.ts:1-37`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/orderingDataService.ts:109-145`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/ordering/orderingDataService.ts:168-186`).
- **Brand presentation:** KFC-named GenUI types/selectors, Vietnamese action labels, KFC-only image-host trust, and KFC-specific customer-language phrases are embedded in presentation code (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/genui/kfcGenUi.ts:1-49`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/genui/kfcGenUi.ts:70-115`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/channelPresentation.ts:130-154`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/src/presentation/customerLanguage.ts:3-94`).
- **Acceptance identity:** dataset/schema/source names, inventory digest/counts, widget-kind types, and scenario catalog are explicitly KFC-owned (`services/kfc-agent-backend/src/evaluation/liveQualityContracts.ts:1-18`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:22-31`).
- **Live-scenario particulars:** item codes, Vietnamese product/modifier names, districts, voucher names, payment brands, loyalty values, and KFC-specific customer utterances are golden compatibility fixtures, not shared-runtime defaults (`services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:27-64`, `services/kfc-agent-backend/test/scenarios/scenarioCoverageLedger.ts:223-260`).

## Migration constraints for behavior compatibility

1. Introduce a trusted server-side **Business Routing Context** before selecting a Business Pack; never let prompt text/model output choose identity authority or cross-tenant state.
2. Move the hard-coded items above behind a KFC Business Pack incrementally, while leaving the reusable ports, provenance envelope, safety gate phase, verified-state reducer, run guards, persistence semantics, and acceptance conjunction intact.
3. Preserve external KFC request/response and channel behavior during extraction: `kfc` remains GenUI; Messenger/Zalo remain standalone projections; Vietnamese customer language and trusted KFC media remain unchanged.
4. Preserve confirmation semantics exactly: catalog/provider revisions and cart/fulfillment/payment state remain bound to order approval; irreversible replay remains atomic and idempotent.
5. Keep planner evidence turn-local, durable state provider-verified, and all customer/personalized hydration scope-authorized. No Business Pack may persist model candidates as facts.
6. Require each provider adapter to return provenance and require each Business Pack to declare capabilities. Unsupported PVCFC capabilities must produce grounded information/clarification/handoff behavior, never a simulated KFC commerce operation.
7. Parameterize schemas by business only where semantics truly differ (currency, locale, content taxonomy, provider operations, lifecycle widgets). Do not weaken shared safety predicates into optional pack conventions.
8. Run the unchanged 92-case KFC gate and GenUI proof as a blocking migration test. A successful compilation, provider mock, or new PVCFC suite is insufficient if any KFC tool/state/presentation/evidence/persistence/latency score regresses.
