## Question

Using TinyFish against `https://www.pvcfc.com.vn/`, what public information must a full-service PVCFC customer assistant know, where is each fact authoritatively published, and what fixture corpus can be captured with durable provenance? Inventory the site's navigable and discoverable content, including company information, products and specifications, crop/agronomic guidance, distribution and contact channels, customer-support entry points and forms, investor materials, sustainability, news, downloadable documents, language variants, and any other customer-relevant class the crawl reveals. Record canonical URLs, titles, language, section/type, publication or update dates where available, extracted text/data, links/documents/media, dynamic or blocked behavior, duplicate/version relationships, and retrieval provenance. Distinguish public knowledge from forms or pages that merely imply unavailable private capabilities. The answer must define the crawl manifest and content taxonomy needed by later Provider, Business Pack, and evaluation decisions; it must not invent private PVCFC API contracts or customer records.
