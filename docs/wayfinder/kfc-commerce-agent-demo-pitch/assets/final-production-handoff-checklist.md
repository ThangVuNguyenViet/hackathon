## Claim gates

Do not introduce any of these during production:

- measured conversion, revenue, latency, productivity, satisfaction, or ROI improvements;
- `39 use cases`, `9/9`, or a blended all-tests-pass number;
- nine current live-model scenarios passing;
- production KFC OMS/POS compatibility or production readiness;
- real KFC order creation or production system-of-record language;
- A2UI, customer text streaming, GenUI structural streaming, or hidden tool-call narration;
- supervisor, specialist-agent, or multi-agent architecture language;
- a claim that the exact three-turn live demo or matching recording is already ready.

5. The matching fallback recording is bound to the same runtime snapshot.
6. The teammate rehearses automatic wrong-state and timeout failover.
7. Thang completes the complete pitch by `4:40` three times.

2. Render and inspect all 13 slides individually at full size.
3. Run overflow QA and remove unintended overlap, wrapping, clipping, placeholders, or mixed numbering.

6. Request and inspect a fresh large thumbnail for every imported slide.
