## Question

What exact hybrid rehearsal script will let Thang deliver the pitch by 4:45 and answer two minutes of technical questions? ## Working decisions

- Spoken pitch and Q&A: English.
- Visible slide copy: English.
- Customer demo submissions: Vietnamese.
- Demo roles: Thang presents and controls the slides; a teammate operates the prepared customer-chat demo and fallback recording.
- Fallback authority: the teammate switches immediately at 18 seconds or on any wrong card, price, cart state, or final outcome. Thang uses the exact line: `I'll switch to the recorded run of this exact scenario.`
- Demo ending: hold the verified confirmed-order result briefly, return to Slide 8, and deliver a 10-15 second close.
- Exact close: `Choose Team Braise to turn KFC conversations into completed orders—while keeping customers and operators in control.`
- Exact opening: `Good morning. We're Team Braise. KFC customers already start ordering in conversation—but completing that order still sends them elsewhere. We built KFC Commerce Agent to keep the customer in chat and carry the journey through to a verified order.`
- Timing: Slide 1 `0:00-0:25`; Slide 2 `0:25-1:00`; Slide 3 `1:00-1:35`; Slide 4A `1:35-2:05`; Slide 4B `2:05-2:35`; Slide 4C `2:35-3:05`; Slide 5 `3:05-3:40`; Slide 6 plus live demo and close `3:40-4:40`; safety `4:40-4:45`.
- Behind-schedule cut order: reduce Slides 4A-4C to one sentence each; skip the Slide 5 evaluation-loop explanation; never cut customer approvals, the confirmed-order reveal, or the exact close.
- Q&A role: Thang answers every question. The teammate operates only the live demo and fallback recording.
