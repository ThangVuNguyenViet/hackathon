## Question

What is the canonical domain contract for a Conversational Transaction from proposed customer intent through identity, authorization, confirmation, execution, reconciliation, human review, and final Action Receipt?

Resolve the boundaries and relationships among Tenant, customer, conversation, channel, business resource, intent revision, Operation Key, action request, confirmation, authorization decision, upstream attempt, Pending Reconciliation, Verified Action, Action Receipt, human ownership, and billable outcome. Define legal state transitions and concrete edge cases for revision, cancellation, duplicate delivery, concurrent requests, stale state, delayed events, partial upstream evidence, and cross-channel continuation without embedding one vendor's schema or promising exactly-once distributed execution.
