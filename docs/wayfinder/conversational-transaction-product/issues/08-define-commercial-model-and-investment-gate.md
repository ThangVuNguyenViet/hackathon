## Question

What validated commercial model makes the product profitable, understandable to enterprise buyers, and honestly comparable with full customer-service platforms, personal-agent runtimes, and custom integration?

Define the fixed 420 million VND pilot milestones and cost model; provisional 60 million VND monthly platform fee; Verified Action billing eligibility; included allowance and volume tiers; connector and enterprise add-ons; infrastructure, model, support, implementation, channel, and upstream pass-through costs; contribution margin; production conversion terms; buyer ROI; partner economics; and the interview, benchmark, and paid-pilot evidence that unlocks full multi-tenant investment. Include explicit kill or reposition criteria if the specialty is already adequately supplied at lower total cost.
