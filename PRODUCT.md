# Product

## Register

product

## Users

KFC Vietnam customers ordering food in a first-party Flutter chat, with the same ordering behavior available through Messenger and Zalo. They need to discover dishes, shape a cart, verify delivery, confirm an order, pay, track it, and request human help without learning an internal operations model.

## Product Purpose

Provide a fast, trustworthy conversational ordering flow grounded in verified KFC menu and commerce data. Structured GenUI should replace long text only when a visual control makes a customer decision clearer.

## Brand Personality

Fast, familiar, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a polished KFC digital counter rather than a generic chatbot or an operator dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Generic chatbot card stacks with repeated headings and actions.
- Oversized controls that make a compact ordering flow feel like an admin panel.
- Inconsistent component sizing between menu, cart, checkout, and payment widgets.
- Decorative or invented food imagery, broken-image placeholders, and unverified media.

## Design Principles

- Keep customer decisions compact and immediately scannable.
- Use one consistent control vocabulary across every ordering stage.
- Show verified product identity, price, quantity, and next action together.
- Let structured controls carry interaction while chat copy stays brief.
- Preserve useful behavior when verified media is unavailable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable Vietnamese typography, meaningful semantics for verified product imagery, keyboard and touch usability, sufficient color contrast, and text-only degradation when media cannot be safely rendered.
